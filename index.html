HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD / arrows or left-thumb joystick. USE (E/Space): do tasks, open the laptop. Crewmates finish all tasks or vote out the Impostor. Impostor: KILL (Q), VENT(V). SCAN (F) shows a temporary minimap with every living player and body (45s cooldown). ROLE (G) uses your special ability and SAB (T) sabotages as Impostor. Esc closes a task, and you can click every button with the mouse. Finish two tasks within 25s for a POWER SURGE speed boost. Every ~40s a STARSHIFT anomaly hits: low gravity, blackout or inverted controls. REPORT (R) a body to call a meeting and vote. Dead players become ghosts: they pass through walls, can only be seen by other ghosts, and can still finish tasks.
